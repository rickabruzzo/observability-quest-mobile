## One painting: `B_clothes.png`, again

The joke of the shot: the pager goes off, the hero gets out of bed, and the nearest clothes are the ones they
took off last night and left on the floor. They flash straight into them and go. So the clothes must look
**dropped, not put away**.

- **The blue jeans** dropped flat and crumpled, the legs askew, one leg bent or turned over.
- **The green hoodie** tossed down beside them and partly over one leg: front side up, one sleeve flung out,
  the hood flopped to one side.
- **The two grey sneakers** kicked off separately, a little apart, one tipped on its side.
- **Nothing folded, nothing stacked, nothing tidy.** Low and wide, with the floor showing between the things.
- Seen from the front and a little above, as the bedroom is.

The same rules as every piece of the painted hero:

- The approved figure's drawing, shading and outline. Attach `art-drops/2026-11-14-hero-painted-test/H_front.png`
  and nothing else.
- Colour families: the hoodie only in greens (its shadows a darker green, not blue-green), the jeans only in
  blues, the sneakers only in neutral greys, **soles light grey**. Nothing in the picture may be yellow or
  cream: yellow is the background, and a sole painted in it comes out as a hole.
- Background one flat pure yellow everywhere, between the garments too: no floor, no rug, no shadow, no other
  object. The room's own rug is under them in the game.
- **Nothing printed on the hoodie.** Where the hoodie lies front up, leave its chest fairly flat and uncovered:
  the game puts the Honeycomb mark there itself, as it does on the figure.
- **At the figure's own scale.** These are the clothes he wears in `H_front.png`, laid down, not shrunk: the
  hoodie about as wide as it is on him. The last painting was at about half his scale, which is part of why it
  read as a small neat stack. The finished picture is about **200 wide × 64 high** on yellow (the game shows it
  at half that, 100×32, and a little smaller again in the bedroom).

Start from this:

> The image is a pixel-art game character painted in flat colour families: a green hoodie, blue jeans, grey
> sneakers, with a soft dark outline. In exactly that drawing style, shading and colour families, draw only his
> clothes from yesterday lying on the floor where he dropped them at bedtime, seen from the front and a little
> above, with no person: the blue jeans dropped flat and crumpled with the legs askew; beside them and partly
> over one leg, the green hoodie tossed down front side up, one sleeve flung out, the hood flopped to one side;
> the two grey sneakers kicked off separately, a little apart, one tipped on its side. Nothing is folded,
> stacked or tidy. The clothes are spread wide and low across the picture, the same size they are on him. The
> hoodie only in tones of green, the jeans only in tones of blue, the sneakers only in neutral greys with light
> grey soles. Nothing printed on the hoodie, no logo. Background: one flat solid pure yellow colour everywhere,
> with no floor, no rug, no shadow and no other object; yellow shows between the garments.
> No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos.

At most 5 tries. **Stop and show Rick the pick** at its real size and at half size before delivering.

Accept when, at half size: you can tell jeans, hoodie and two shoes apart at a glance; nothing looks folded or
stacked; it reads as last night's clothes on the floor; each garment is in its own colour family with no yellow
inside it.
